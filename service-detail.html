Turnkey Civil Contracting & Execution

Translating 3D photorealistic BIM models into flawless structural reality across Ludhiana & Amritsar.

Step-by-Step Execution Workflow

Our proprietary 3D-to-Civil methodology eliminates on-site guesswork. Led by Udhay Seth (Principal Civil Constructor) and Mudita D Seth (Lead Interior Designer), every wall beam, MEP duct, and cove detail is pre-calculated in 3D photorealism before concrete pouring begins.

01

3D Spatial BIM Modeling

Constructing a millimeter-accurate 3D digital twin of your villa, including lighting raytracing and material texture simulations.

02

RCC Bare Shell Civil Audit

Laser surveying bare brick structure on-site in Ludhiana/Amritsar to align structural tolerances before MEP work.

03

Material Curation & Hardware Sourcing

Importing travertine marble slabs, fluted oak joinery, and Blumotion hardware directly from authorized Italian & German suppliers.

04

On-Site Civil Execution & Handover

Managing turnkey masonry, plumbing, acoustic isolation, and joinery installation until 100% render match is handed over.
