Architectural Blueprint & Civil Narrative

At NAM Atelier, every structural element is calculated in 3D photorealism before site groundbreaking begins. This residence features double-height cantilever ceilings, concealed HVAC slot diffusers, and monolithic stone flooring engineered to withstand Punjab's thermal cycles.

Custom Italian joinery was sourced with German Blum hardware integration. Civil execution on-site achieved 100% geometric alignment with the approved 3D VR walkthrough.
