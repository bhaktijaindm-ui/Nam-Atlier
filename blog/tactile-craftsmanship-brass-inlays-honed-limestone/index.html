Tactile Craftsmanship: Champagne Brass Inlays & Honed Limestone

Exploring shadow reveal detailing, brushed metal accents, and micro-bevel joints in luxury interior carpentry.

In true architectural luxury, greatness resides in the micro-details: where two distinct materials meet, how a drawer pulls, and the precise width of a shadow reveal.

1. The 5mm Negative Shadow Reveal

Rather than relying on heavy traditional skirting boards or decorative cornices, we design 5mm recessed black shadow reveals at the junction of floor, wall, and ceiling. This imparts lightness, making walls appear to hover above the stone floor.

2. Brushed Champagne Brass Transition Trims

Where honed travertine meets fluted dark walnut cabinetry, we embed 3mm solid brushed champagne brass inlays. Over time, the brass develops a subtle patina, celebrating natural material authenticity and artisanal precision.

3. Mitred 45-Degree Stone Edges

All visible stone edges on vanity tops, island counters, and staircase risers are precision-cut at 45-degree angles and epoxied with color-matched resin, creating crisp solid blocks that honor the stone's natural veining.
