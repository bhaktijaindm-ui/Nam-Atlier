The master bedroom suite is the ultimate personal sanctuary within the home. Its design demands a heightened sensitivity to acoustic tranquility, tactile softness, and restorative lighting.

1. Acoustic Bedhead Paneling

Behind the custom king platform bed, we introduce floor-to-ceiling acoustic wall upholstery in soft bouclÃ© or brushed wool, framed in subtle champagne brass trims. This absorbs flutter echoes, creating a serene, hushed atmosphere for deep rest.

2. Concealed Dressing & Spa En-Suite Flow

Wardrobes are organized into dedicated dressing suites with smoked glass fronts and internal warm LED motion sensors. The flow continues into a spa bathroom lined in monolithic honed marble, featuring a freestanding soaking tub and rain shower enclave.

3. Low-Level Nightlight Coves

Recessed 2200K warm LED guide strips beneath bed plinths and along bathroom baseboards allow safe navigation at night without activating harsh overhead lamps.
