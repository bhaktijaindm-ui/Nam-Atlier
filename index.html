Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our turnkey design methodology, 3D walkthroughs, and material sourcing.

Our turnkey projects range between 6 to 14 months, depending on scope and customization depth. Phase 1 (Architectural 3D VR & Spatial Planning) takes 4-6 weeks, followed by precise material procurement and millwork crafting before site assembly.

Before touching a single brick, we create a 1:1 photorealistic digital twin of your property. You can explore your residence in virtual reality, test natural lighting at different hours of the day, and evaluate custom marble veining and velvet textures in real-time.

We source structural marble directly from premier quarries in Carrara (Italy) and Makrana (Rajasthan). All custom joinery uses solid dark oak, fluted walnut, and German Blum/Hettich concealed soft-close hardware engineered for lifetime durability.
