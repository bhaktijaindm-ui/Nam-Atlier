Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting your architectural interior project.

A full turnkey luxury villa interior & civil execution typically ranges from 4 to 8 months depending on square footage, material customization, and structural masonry requirements. 3D renders are finalized in the first 3 weeks.

We craft 4K photorealistic 3D models of every room including lighting simulations, furniture ergonomics, and material swatches. You can virtually walk through your entire residence before a single brick is laid on-site.

We source authentic imported Italian Statuario & Travertine marble directly from Kishangarh/Italy, premium Seasoned Teakwood, and official German Blum & Hafele hardware for soft-close joinery.

Udhay Seth oversees a comprehensive BOQ (Bill of Quantities) broken down line by line before work starts. We maintain zero hidden costs with structured milestone payment schedules.
