ACOUSTIC PHYSICS & IMMERSION

The Engineering of Private Screening Sanctuaries

A true luxury private screening room is fundamentally an exercise in acoustic physics, psychoacoustics, and spatial ergonomics. Simply purchasing expensive AV electronics and mounting them on finished walls never yields studio-quality performance; without scientific acoustic treatment, modal room resonances, muddy bass buildup, and harsh flutter echoes destroy dialogue clarity and sonic immersion. At NAM Atelier, we engineer private cinemas from the bare RCC shell outward, delivering visceral reference-grade acoustic isolation and cinema-standard playback.

Room-Within-a-Room Structural Decoupling

High-output subwoofers generate low-frequency sound waves (20Hz to 80Hz) that easily travel through solid concrete slabs and steel beams as structure-borne vibration, disturbing family members in adjoining bedrooms or study suites. To eliminate this, we build a decoupled "room-within-a-room." We cast a floating concrete floor resting on calibrated Sylomer acoustic elastomeric pads, build independent stud walls decoupled from the structural shell via resilient isolation clips, and hang a multi-layer acoustic ceiling from spring vibration isolators. Low-frequency energy is mechanically absorbed, ensuring zero sound transmission outside the theatre.

Dolby Atmos 9.4.4 Spatial Calibration & Concealed Baffles

Our screening rooms are engineered around immersive 3D spatial audio formats including Dolby Atmos, Auro-3D, and IMAX Enhanced. We calculate exact speaker placement angles, screen-distance ratios, and listener sweet-spots based on room volumetric dimensions. All surround, height, and front screen-channel speakers are concealed behind acoustically transparent woven fabrics from Guildford of Maine, framed by architectural fluted timber fins and bronze reveals. Behind these elegant finishes lie custom-engineered bass traps, binary amplitude diffusers, and broadband absorption panels tuned to achieve a target reverberation time (RT60) of 0.25 to 0.35 seconds across all frequencies.

Micro-Perforated Woven Screens & 4K Laser Projection

Visual fidelity must equal acoustic perfection. We install premium micro-perforated woven projection screens from Stewart Filmscreen and Severtson, allowing behind-screen center-channel speakers to fire sound directly through the picture without high-frequency attenuation or moiré artifacts. Paired with high-lumen native 4K laser projectors from Barco, Sony, and Christie housed in isolated projector hush-boxes, our theatres deliver breathtaking HDR contrast, velvety cinematic black levels, and razor-sharp color rendition.

CLIENT INQUIRIES

Acoustic Cinema Frequently Asked Questions

What room dimensions are best suited for a private luxury cinema?

A minimum space of approximately 18 feet by 24 feet with a 10.5-foot clear ceiling height provides an ideal volumetric canvas for a two-tier screening room accommodating 8 to 12 luxury recliners. We strictly avoid square room dimensions (such as 20x20 ft) as they create severe modal standing wave overlap that distorts bass frequencies.

Can the cinema also be used for karaoke, gaming, and watching sports with ambient lights on?

Yes. We program custom automated scenes into the room controller: "Cinema Mode" turns off all lights and dims the starlight ceiling; "Sports & Social Mode" engages subtle floor coves and accentuates center dialogue for commentary while keeping low ambient light for drinks and dining; and "Karaoke Mode" recalibrates microphone audio through dedicated PA processing to protect high-end cinema speakers.

What brands of audio and projection hardware do you integrate?

We remain brand-agnostic to select the absolute best equipment for your room volume and budget, regularly integrating Meyer Sound, JBL Synthesis, Wisdom Audio, and Bowers & Wilkins Diamond speakers paired with StormAudio, Trinnov Audio, and Anthem processors, alongside Barco Residential and Sony 4K laser projectors.

How easy is it for family members to operate the system?

We eliminate the dreaded collection of six different remotes. The entire cinema—projector, audio processors, Apple TV, Kaleidescape movie server, lighting scenes, and motorized reclining seats—is controlled via an intuitive custom iPad or Savant/Crestron touch panel. Tapping "Play Movie" dims the lights, starts the projector, adjusts audio levels, and begins playback automatically.
