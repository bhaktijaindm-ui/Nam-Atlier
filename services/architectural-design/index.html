OUR DISCIPLINE

Architectural Philosophy & Site Strategy

At NAM Atelier, architectural planning is an exercise in timeless proportion, structural honesty, and functional clarity. Led by Udhay Seth and Mudita D Seth, every villa design begins with site sun-path orientation, prevailing breeze corridors, and family lifestyle zoning.

We combine dramatic architectural cantilevers with monolithic natural stone cladding, double-height insulated Low-E glass walls, and deep overhangs that shield interiors from extreme summer heat while capturing low winter sun.
