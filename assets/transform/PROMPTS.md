# NAM ATELIER — "Raw → Bespoke" Image Generation Prompts

## Technical Specifications
- **Format:** 3:2 landscape (at least 2400×1600)
- **Style:** Real architectural documentary photography for an Indian residential project (apartment/villa, Indore/Punjab construction). Shot on Sony A7R IV, 24mm tilt-shift lens, f/8, tripod, eye-level (1.2m), straight verticals.
- **Lighting:** Natural daylight from windows only, soft late-morning light, subtle film grain, true-to-life color, no HDR glow, no oversaturation, no lens flare.
- **Imperfections:** Slight shadow falloff, natural material texture, tiny cable bundles, real construction dust in raw shots; lived-in styling details in luxury shots (slightly folded throw, open art book, real indoor plant, natural curtain folds).
- **Negative Prompt (All):** CGI, 3D render, illustration, plastic look, over-smooth surfaces, fisheye, warped geometry, extra legs on furniture, melted objects, gibberish text, oversaturated, HDR, neon, stock-photo glossy, watermark, people.
