Luxury Kitchen and Dining

Black and Brass Kitchen

Moody Editorial Elegance in Nero Marquina, Smoked Glass and Gold
Location Delhi, India
Year Completed 2024
Enclosed Area 3,100 SQ FT
Execution Duration 10 Months
Architectural Service Luxury Kitchen and Dining

“Created within an expansive Lutyens-adjacent residence in New Delhi, the Black and Brass Kitchen is a dramatic culinary salon informed by European bistro culture and bespoke cabinetmaking. Clad in leathered Nero Marquina marble, patinated solid brass, and smoked bronze glass vitrines, the kitchen elevates culinary craft into moody, nocturnal luxury.”

The Brief

Located within a private diplomatic villa in New Delhi, the client requested a dramatic departure from typical sterile white kitchen designs. As passionate epicures and collectors of rare single malts, the homeowners envisioned a deeply atmospheric, nocturnal kitchen that would function as a stylish cocktail lounge and interactive cooking salon during late-night gatherings.

The brief required an uncompromising high-performance workstation, a dedicated walk-in sommelier wine repository, a barista coffee bar with commercial plumbing, and a private dining booth inspired by grand historic European bistros.

Furthermore, the patrons requested that all kitchen joinery feel like custom library furniture rather than utilitarian kitchen cabinetry, demanding integrated concealed appliances and hand-finished patinated metals throughout.

The Challenge

Executing a predominantly dark, moody interior without making the space feel oppressive or cramped was the central spatial challenge. The room received natural light primarily from north-facing courtyards, requiring sophisticated optical balancing of matte, gloss, and translucent surfaces to bounce daylight without creating distracting reflections.

Additionally, the installation of floor-to-ceiling smoked bronze vitrines required custom vibration-dampened steel suspension frames to withstand the micro-vibrations of heavy commercial refrigeration compressors concealed in adjacent utility service shafts.

Managing ambient dust and heat dissipation from specialized refrigeration units in Delhi's intense summer climate required engineering continuous subterranean fresh-air ventilation intake shafts to ensure equipment longevity.

The Concept

Our design concept, 'The Alchemist's Salon', treats cooking and cocktail craft as sensory alchemy. We composed the interior through an interplay of deep obsidian tones, warm reflective metals, and amber lighting layers. The space embraces dark richness: black leathered stone grounds the room, smoked glass vitrines provide optical depth, and solid brushed brass fixtures act as warm jewelry.

The spatial layout centers around a monolithic black marble island with an integrated sunken prep channel, allowing hosts to face guests seated at the low-height brass bar counter while orchestrating dinners.

By employing deep shadow and warm point-source lighting, the kitchen takes on the theatrical intimacy of a historic private club, making every culinary task feel ritualistic and celebratory.

Space by Space

Upon stepping through the arched steel portal from the grand gallery, guests encounter the primary culinary salon. The centerpiece island is crafted from Nero Marquina marble, its matte textured surface silken to the touch. Behind it, a magnificent 4.8-meter-high display wall combines smoked glass cabinetry backlit with 2200K amber LEDs, showcasing handcrafted glassware and copper cookware.

To the left, a walk-in temperature-controlled wine room holds 750 bottles displayed horizontally on walnut dowels. To the right, an intimate breakfast booth lined in button-tufted black saddle leather offers a cozy retreat for morning espresso and newspaper reading.

Behind the main display wall, a concealed butler's pantry and heavy scullery handles dishwashing and dish staging, keeping the main entertaining island spotless throughout formal dinner parties.

Materials and Finishes

Every surface was curated for tactile density and patinating beauty. We imported Spanish Nero Marquina marble with crisp white calcite veins and applied a bespoke river-wash leathering technique that gives the stone a warm, tactile skin texture rather than cold high-gloss polish.

Custom millwork is built from American ash stained to a deep charcoal tint that preserves the open wood grain. All metal elements—including the pot rails, island footrests, cabinet pulls, and tapware—are machined from solid unlacquered brass, intentionally left unprotected so they develop an authentic, touch-burnished patina over years of culinary use.

Flooring is paved with Belgian bluestone slabs finished with a soft flamed texture that resists oil stains and provides an earthy, grounded mineral counterpoint to the dark cabinetry.

Light and Atmosphere

Lighting was developed as a theatrical production. Concealed linear profiles beneath cabinet plinths and shelf overhangs wash vertical surfaces with low-glare warmth. Inside the smoked glass vitrines, micro-optical fiber lights highlight glassware edges, creating glowing amber jewel boxes against dark walls.

During daytime hours, sheer bronze mesh blinds filter incoming daylight into a soft, diffused golden haze. At night, the room transforms into an intimate speakeasy where the only illuminated planes are the workstations, the wine collection, and the faces of friends gathered around the counter.

Task lighting above the preparation counters features narrow 20-degree optical beams with honeycomb louvers, keeping light strictly focused on cutting boards while keeping surrounding seating areas in warm shadow.

Craft and Execution

The bespoke brass handles were individually CNC-machined from solid brass billet stock, hand-buffed with pumice stone, and fitted with silent magnetic catches. The stone island features a continuous 45-degree mitered edge detail with book-matched calcite veining flowing uninterrupted from top surfaces down the waterfall end panels.

To guarantee spotless maintenance, all internal drawers were fitted with custom walnut dividers lined with Italian microfiber, while grease extraction is managed by a recessed ceiling-flush induction capture hood operating at sub-45dB noise levels.

The wine cellar's climate envelope was constructed with dual vapor barriers and specialized insulated glass units, maintaining a constant 14°C temperature and 65% humidity to preserve fragile vintage labels.

The Result

The Black and Brass Kitchen is a striking celebration of moody luxury and bespoke engineering. It challenges the conventional wisdom that domestic kitchens must be sterile white spaces, demonstrating that rich, dark materiality and warm lighting can create an infinitely more hospitable, intimate environment.

It represents an architectural triumph for NAM Atelier in Delhi—a culinary salon that effortlessly transitions from functional morning preparation to sophisticated nocturnal hospitality.

The patrons describe it as the single most captivating room in their estate, where guests inevitably gather and linger long into the night.
