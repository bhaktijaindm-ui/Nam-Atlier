The Whisky Library

A Clubby Nocturnal Retreat in Smoked Walnut, Leather and Amber Light
Location Pune, India
Year Completed 2024
Enclosed Area 1,800 SQ FT
Execution Duration 6 Months
Architectural Service Home Theatre and Bar

“Created for a distinguished collector in Pune, The Whisky Library is an 1,800-square-foot private tasting salon and intimate cigar lounge. Clad in book-matched smoked American walnut, custom brass bottle vitrines, and button-tufted cognac leather seating, the room evokes the timeless intimacy of historic Scottish distillery cellars and private London gentlemen's clubs.”

The Brief

The client, a prominent Pune industrialist and passionate collector of rare single malt Scotch and Japanese whiskies, commissioned NAM Atelier to transform an unused annex into an intimate private tasting salon, library, and walk-in cigar humidor. The space was envisioned as a refined venue for slow conversations, business negotiations, and contemplative evening tastings.

Requirements included secure, temperature-monitored display shelving for over 450 rare bottles, an independent positive-pressure smoke extraction system for the cigar lounge, and a dedicated wet bar equipped with specialized clear-ice carving stations.

The patron stressed that the space must feel authentic and historic: no modern plastic laminates, no cold LED downlights, and no commercial bar flashiness—only rich, dark, natural materials that celebrate the heritage of fine spirits.

The Challenge

Cigar lounges present severe environmental challenges: cigar smoke contains dense oils and odors that can permanently penetrate upholstery, woodwork, and books unless airflow is continuously managed. The HVAC system needed to introduce 100% fresh, conditioned air with rapid extraction without creating noisy air currents or drafts that would disturb cigar embers.

Additionally, the storage of vintage spirits requires strict protection from ultraviolet light and thermal cycling to prevent evaporation, cork deterioration, and flavor loss over decades of aging.

Acoustic isolation was also vital: ensuring that late-night tasting sessions and music would not carry into adjacent residential suites across the courtyard.

The Concept

Our design concept, 'The Amber Vault', draws upon the sensory atmosphere of historic barrel aging cellars. We utilized a monochromatic, tactile envelope of dark smoked walnut, honed black soapstone, and aged brass that absorbs light and directs focus to the luminous golden hues of the whisky bottles.

The room is organized into two connected zones: a tasting library centered around a low circular cocktail table and leather club chairs, and an adjoining walk-in cigar humidor separated by airtight smoked bronze glass partitions.

By employing deep shadow and warm point-source lighting, the room takes on the timeless, comforting gravity of a historic Scottish library.

Space by Space

Entry is through a heavy four-inch-thick walnut pivot door with an integrated brass drop seal. The main tasting salon features three-meter-high walnut shelving arrays with micro-perforated brass uprights. Low-slung armchairs upholstered in vintage-waxed cognac leather invite unhurried conversations.

At the center, a tasting bar carved from a single slab of Indian black soapstone includes an integrated water font and recessed brass drain troughs for water drops during tastings. The adjacent humidor is lined entirely in untreated Spanish cedar, housing custom roller drawers and digital hygrometer instrumentation.

A private card corner features a circular walnut table with green baize inlay, illuminated by a low-hanging brass dome pendant.

Materials and Finishes

Materials were chosen for tactile warmth and authentic patination. Smoked American walnut was subjected to deep thermal vaporization, imparting a rich, dark espresso tone throughout the timber without surface stains. Countertops utilize Indian black soapstone, a heat-resistant, chemically inert stone with a silken, soap-like hand feel.

Upholstery features full-grain aniline saddle leather hand-waxed with beeswax to highlight natural hide characteristics. Metal details were machined from solid brass billet stock and chemically aged to a warm, antique olive-bronze finish.

Flooring is finished in hand-planed smoked oak parquet laid in a classic herringbone pattern, treated with natural beeswax to impart a subtle, woody fragrance to the room.

Light and Atmosphere

Illumination was tuned to match the spectrum of candle flames and amber spirits. Every shelf features concealed 2200K optical light guides with high color rendering, edge-lighting the glass bottles from below so the liquor glows warmly like molten topaz.

Overhead downlights were completely eliminated from seating zones. Low-level bronze floor fixtures and a dimmable table lamp with an oiled parchment shade provide all task illumination, wrapping the room in cinematic, clubby shadows.

The cigar humidor features warm amber halo grazing that highlights the natural grain of the Spanish cedar shelving without generating heat.

Craft and Execution

The cigar humidor was constructed with multi-layered vapor barriers and lined with 25mm solid Spanish cedar to naturally regulate humidity at sixty-eight percent. The smoke evacuation system employs a dedicated laminar-flow ceiling exhaust plenum coupled with carbon-activated electrostatic filters, completely clearing the room of smoke within four minutes.

Bespoke joinery incorporated silent magnetic latching systems and internal concealed wiring conduits, ensuring that no technical hardware or light transformers are visible throughout the millwork.

The soapstone counter was cut from a single quarry block and hand-honed with wet diamond pads, creating an impervious surface that resists alcohol and citrus spills without chemical sealers.

The Result

The Whisky Library has become the definitive private retreat for its owner. It achieves a rare balance of clubby historic romance and modern climate engineering, offering an intoxicating sanctuary for connoisseurship, conversation, and quiet contemplation.

It showcases NAM Atelier’s mastery in creating atmospheric, single-purpose luxury spaces tailored to the refined passions of our patrons.

The client considers it the crown jewel of his residence—a warm, amber sanctuary that deepens in character and memories with every shared bottle.
