Staircase, Foyer and Facade

The Spiral Foyer

A Cantilevered Helical Masterpiece of Sculpted Stone and Curved Glass
Location Indore, India
Year Completed 2025
Enclosed Area 3,800 SQ FT
Execution Duration 10 Months
Architectural Service Staircase, Foyer and Facade

“Serving as the grand ceremonial entrance of a modern neoclassical estate in Indore, The Spiral Foyer is a double-height architectural volume anchored by an unsupported helical staircase. Engineered in post-tensioned reinforced concrete, honed beige marble treads, and curved structural glass balustrades, the foyer turns arrival into a dynamic spatial crescendo.”

The Brief

Commissioned for a sprawling private estate in Indore, the patrons requested an awe-inspiring ceremonial arrival foyer that would set the tone for the entire residence. The centerpiece was to be an unsupported, sweeping helical staircase that appeared to float effortlessly through a nine-meter double-height volume without visible intermediate support columns.

The foyer had to effortlessly manage the arrival of up to two hundred guests during formal family receptions, provide seamless circulation into formal dining and garden wings, and maintain exceptional acoustic clarity despite its grand, cavernous volume.

The family insisted on complete structural rigidity: the staircase could exhibit zero bounce, creak, or perceptible vibration when ascending or descending, demanding extraordinary structural engineering.

The Challenge

Designing a freestanding helical staircase spanning nine vertical meters without central columns or perimeter wall ties requires extraordinary structural and civil engineering. Cantilevered torsional forces and dynamic bounce under heavy foot traffic had to be eliminated to ensure that the staircase feels rock-solid underfoot.

Furthermore, fabricating and installing continuous curved laminated glass balustrades with compound three-dimensional radii required laser-scanned digital templating and high-temperature autoclave bending to avoid optical distortion or stress fractures.

Acoustic reverberation in a nine-meter-tall volume clad in marble posed a major conversational barrier, requiring advanced acoustic absorption solutions hidden within ceiling coffering.

The Concept

Our architectural concept, 'The Ascending Ribbon', treats the staircase as a dynamic sculptural ribbon unwinding through space. Rather than a bulky classical stone stair, we designed a slender post-tensioned concrete structural spine clad in warm Italian Botticino marble, paired with an ethereal transparent glass balustrade and a continuous hand-sculpted bronze railing.

The double-height arrival hall is composed around classical proportions, featuring deep arched stone colonnades that frame views through to a lush central courtyard, anchoring the vertical ascent with grounded horizontal axes.

The spatial choreography invites the visitor on a fluid upward journey, with changing vistas of interior art and exterior gardens revealed at every turn of the spiral.

Space by Space

Guests arrive through four-meter-tall bronze-clad pivot doors into an expansive stone foyer. The floor is laid in large-format Botticino Fiorito marble, bordered by subtle 3mm solid brass inlay lines that guide circulation toward the sweeping staircase.

As one ascends the cantilevered treads, the curved glass balustrade reveals unfolding perspectives of the central courtyard and an eight-meter-tall bespoke crystal and bronze chandelier suspended from the coffered ceiling. The upper mezzanine bridge floats across the foyer, connecting the east and west bedroom wings.

Beneath the spiral, a sunken circular reflecting pool lined with black basalt water weir creates gentle rippling reflections across the underside of the ascending marble treads.

Materials and Finishes

The architectural palette balances lightness with classical permanence. Italian Botticino Fiorito marble was selected for its warm ivory ground and delicate golden-amber veins, honed to a velvety, slip-resistant texture. Stair treads feature concealed 3mm brass nosing inlays that provide visual edge definition.

The balustrade is crafted from 21.5mm curved low-iron laminated structural glass clamped into concealed base shoes. The continuous handrail was hand-sculpted from solid architectural bronze, polished and chemically treated to a warm antique finish.

The coffered ceiling was surfaced in seamless acoustic plaster that absorbs sound waves while preserving a smooth, monolithic appearance.

Light and Atmosphere

Lighting was designed as an architectural performance. Each individual marble stair tread features a concealed underside LED channel that washes the riser below with warm 2400K illumination, making the entire helical structure appear to levitate on a bed of golden light after dark.

By day, tall double-height arched windows flood the foyer with natural northern light, while the acoustic plaster ceiling eliminates flutter echoes, ensuring that arrival conversations remain crisp, warm, and intimate.

At night, the monumental central chandelier is dimmed down to warm amber point-sources, transforming the double-height volume into a luminous, star-lit celestial chamber.

Craft and Execution

Executing the staircase required specialized post-tensioned reinforced concrete civil casting. High-tensile steel cables were threaded through curved internal conduits in the concrete spiral and tensioned with hydraulic jacks, pre-stressing the structure to achieve zero deflection and absolute rigidity under load.

The curved glass panels were mapped using 3D LiDAR point-cloud scanners and custom-bent in a high-precision kiln, resulting in smooth, continuous curvature with tolerances under 0.8 millimeters across the entire fifteen-meter helical sweep.

The bronze handrail was cast in sequential curved sections, brazed on-site, and hand-filed to produce one continuous, seamless tactile ribbon that guides the hand effortlessly up the stair.

The Result

The Spiral Foyer is an undisputed architectural triumph in Indore, uniting cutting-edge structural physics with timeless luxury craft. It transforms everyday arrival into a memorable, breathtaking spatial ceremony.

It exemplifies NAM Atelier’s capacity to execute high-complexity civil engineering challenges with poise, elegance, and unyielding aesthetic discipline.

The estate patrons report that the staircase has become the visual signature of their home—a timeless sculptural centerpiece admired by architects and guests from around the country.
