Bathroom and Spa

The Marble Bath

Classical Radiance in Book-Matched Calacatta and Brushed Gold
Location Indore, India
Year Completed 2023
Enclosed Area 1,400 SQ FT
Execution Duration 6 Months
Architectural Service Bathroom and Spa

“Designed for a grand private residence in Indore, The Marble Bath is an exercise in classical proportion and luminous materiality. Cloaked entirely in book-matched Italian Calacatta Michelangelo marble slabs, with solid brass fittings and an arched natural light atrium, the master bathroom balances timeless European elegance with modern spatial serenity.”

The Brief

The patrons, connoisseurs of classical Italian architecture, commissioned NAM Atelier to create a primary master bathroom within their Indore villa that would embody the serene grandeur of classic Tuscan villas. The space needed to feel bathed in natural light, expansive, and immaculately tailored, avoiding trendy finishes in favor of enduring classical stone architecture.

The programmatic requirements called for generous his-and-hers vanity stations with extensive concealed cosmetics storage, an oval soaking tub positioned as a visual centerpiece, a private enclosed water closet, and a walk-in shower with integrated bench seating.

The homeowners specifically requested that the stone surfaces appear luminous and continuous, without chaotic tile patterns, ensuring that the natural veining of the marble would flow gracefully across floors, walls, and vanity counters.

The Challenge

The main architectural challenge was vein-matching across twenty-eight individual large-format Italian marble slabs covering floors, walls, vanity countertops, and shower enclosures. A single misalignment of three millimeters would disrupt the continuous fluid rhythm of the natural stone grain across corner transitions.

Additionally, the room received daylight through an overhead architectural light atrium, which had to be engineered to prevent midday heat gain while filtering natural daylight into a soft, flattering, shadow-free glow across the vanity mirrors.

Moisture resistance was also paramount: natural marble can absorb water and develop yellowing oxidation if improperly sealed, requiring specialized anti-efflorescence sub-slab treatments.

The Concept

Our conceptual direction, 'The Luminous Sanctuary', celebrates white stone as a vessel for light. We utilized extra-choice Calacatta Michelangelo marble, renowned for its luminous crystalline white background and delicate, feathered grey-and-gold veining, creating an ethereal, floating atmosphere.

The spatial layout is anchored along a strong central axis: upon entry, the eye is drawn past the floating double vanities to the freestanding oval tub positioned beneath the arched daylight atrium, establishing classical symmetry and visual balance.

By floating the heavy stone vanities and utilizing frameless glass partitions, the design achieves an uplifting sense of weightlessness within a space constructed from heavy mineral stone.

Space by Space

The double vanity console floats effortlessly above the honed marble floor, carved from solid slabs of Calacatta with undermount basins and wall-mounted brass spouts. Above, two custom arched mirrors framed in unlacquered brass feature concealed perimeter halo lighting that illuminates the face with zero shadows.

In the center, an ergonomic composite stone oval tub rests on a slightly raised stone plinth. To the rear, frameless Starphire glass doors lead to the expansive shower enclosure on the left, lined with non-slip honed Thassos marble, and the discreet private water closet on the right.

A concealed beauty station features integrated power sockets inside velvet-lined oak drawers, keeping all grooming appliances completely out of sight.

Materials and Finishes

The defining material is Calacatta Michelangelo marble, personally inspected and tagged at the quarry in Carrara, Italy. Slabs were finished with a satin hone that diffuses reflections and resists moisture etching.

All fittings—from the thermostatic shower mixers to the towel warmers and cabinet handles—are machined from solid brass and treated with an artisanal brushed gold satin finish. Vanity drawers are crafted from solid American white oak with dovetail joinery, lined in moisture-sealed vegan leather.

Ceilings were finished with breathable Italian micro-cement, tinted to match the soft off-white mineral background of the marble slabs.

Light and Atmosphere

Daylight is gathered through an overhead light atrium fitted with motorized solar louvers that track the sun, ensuring continuous soft, indirect daylight throughout the day without solar heat buildup.

Artificial lighting is meticulously layered: 98 CRI vanity side sconces provide perfect natural color rendering for grooming, while floor-level grazing lights beneath the vanity and tub plinth create a romantic, low-glare amber ambiance for relaxing evening soaks.

At twilight, the ceiling atrium glows with subtle indigo ambient light, harmonizing with the warm 2200K interior cove lighting to create a soothing twilight bath atmosphere.

Craft and Execution

To achieve flawless book-matching, every marble slab was scanned into high-resolution 3D software before water-jet cutting. Stone pieces were dry-laid on the studio floor in Ludhiana to verify millimeter-precise vein alignment before being transported and installed on-site in Indore.

Underfloor heating mats were installed beneath the entire marble floor plate, providing gentle, even warmth on cool winter mornings and accelerating floor drying to maintain spotless, slip-free surfaces.

All stone joints were sealed with micro-porous, color-matched silane-modified polymer grouts that flex with structural shifts, preventing unsightly hairline cracking.

The Result

The Marble Bath is a radiant masterwork of classical proportion and stone craftsmanship. It captures the timeless poetry of European luxury while functioning with the effortless convenience and mechanical precision of twenty-first-century civil contracting.

It represents an enduring triumph in NAM Atelier’s portfolio, offering its patrons a luminous, uplifting start to every single day.

The clients describe stepping into the room each morning as an inspiring, transformative ritual that sets a serene tone for the entire day.
