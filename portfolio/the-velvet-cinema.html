The Velvet Cinema

Dolby Atmos Acoustic Precision Wrapped in Olive Mohair Velvet
Location Delhi, India
Year Completed 2025
Enclosed Area 2,200 SQ FT
Execution Duration 7 Months
Architectural Service Home Theatre and Bar

“Built inside a premier private residence in South Delhi, The Velvet Cinema is a twelve-seat private screening room and cocktail lounge engineered to commercial reference standards. Wrapped in custom acoustic wall panels upholstered in deep olive mohair velvet, ribbed walnut joinery, and an integrated bar, the space delivers cinematic immersion with five-star luxury.”

The Brief

The client, a prominent Bollywood film producer and cinema connoisseur, commissioned NAM Atelier to build an uncompromising private cinema in his South Delhi villa. The facility had to deliver commercial reference-level Dolby Atmos 9.4.6 audio and 4K laser projection capable of screening uncompressed DCI cinema masters, while avoiding the tacky 'spaceship' or sterile corporate aesthetics common to high-end home theaters.

The space required twelve luxurious motorized recliners arranged across three stepped tiers, along with a full-service hospitality cocktail bar at the rear for post-screening discussions.

Crucially, the client requested that all electronic equipment, surround speakers, subwoofers, and acoustic treatment panels be completely hidden from sight, allowing pure architectural materials and luxurious textiles to dominate the space.

The Challenge

The theatre was situated in the basement level directly beneath the villa’s primary master bedroom suite. Ensuring zero acoustic bass transmission at sound pressure levels exceeding 105 decibels required constructing a completely decoupled 'room-within-a-room' floating structure.

Furthermore, achieving ideal reverberation decay times (RT60 of 0.28 seconds) across all audio frequencies without making the room sound 'dead' or boxy demanded an intricate balance of concealed bass traps, resonant membrane absorbers, and quadratic residue diffusers disguised behind seamless architectural upholstery.

Sub-floor thermal insulation was also critical to prevent basement humidity from affecting the electronic amplification racks and delicate projection lenses.

The Concept

Our aesthetic direction, 'Nocturnal Velvet', draws inspiration from classic mid-century European cinema palaces, reimagined through clean architectural geometry. We wrapped the room in deep olive mohair velvet, rich fluted walnut, and brushed brass details that create a warm, sensual atmosphere even when the screen is powered down.

All technical equipment—including fifteen surround speakers, four high-excursion subwoofers, and acoustic treatment panels—was completely concealed behind acoustically transparent fabric walls, allowing pure architecture and luxurious textiles to define the experience.

The spatial layout seamlessly marries acoustic physics with hospitality: a full-service cocktail bar at the rear allows guests to refresh drinks without leaving the cinematic environment.

Space by Space

Entry is via a pressurized sound-lock vestibule featuring acoustic seals and a low-lit brass signage niche. Inside, twelve custom recliners upholstered in olive mohair velvet occupy three stepped tiers, each equipped with an integrated brass cocktail side table and silent motorized recline controls.

At the rear of the theater, the floor rises to the cocktail bar platform. Here, a curved bar counter in Saint Laurent marble and fluted walnut joinery provides wine chillers, crystal glassware storage, and an espresso station for intermissions.

The projection screen is a 180-inch micro-perforated acoustically transparent curved display, behind which the primary front screen channels and twin subwoofers deliver dialogue directly through the picture plane.

Materials and Finishes

The primary wall covering is custom-loomed olive mohair velvet, selected for its luxurious sheen, durability, and natural high-frequency sound-absorbing properties. The timber elements are crafted from dark American walnut, treated with deep matte oils.

The bar counter features honed Saint Laurent black marble with golden veins, complemented by unlacquered antique brass foot rails. The floor is lined with high-density acoustic underlayment beneath custom 1200-gram pure wool carpeting designed to absorb footfall noise.

Ceiling treatments utilize dark charcoal acoustic plaster that absorbs stray light scatter from the screen while preventing sound flutter.

Light and Atmosphere

Lighting is calibrated with three distinct operational scenes: Reception, Screening, and Cocktail Hour. During reception, warm 2200K cove grazing accents the fluted walnut walls and the marble bar counter.

During screenings, all illumination fades to absolute black, leaving only micro-step low-level LED safety runners operating at less than 1% brightness to allow safe movement without casting stray light onto the 180-inch micro-perforated projection screen.

At the end of a film, the lighting smoothly fades up over eight seconds to a warm candlelit glow, ensuring eyes adjust gently to ambient light.

Craft and Execution

Civil construction involved pouring a 100mm reinforced concrete floating slab isolated from the main foundation by heavy-duty Sylomer elastomer springs, creating a mechanical barrier that eliminates sub-bass structural vibration.

The projection booth was isolated in a separate acoustic chamber behind double-glazed optical glass, silencing projector cooling fans. The HVAC system utilized oversized low-velocity sound attenuators, achieving an ultra-quiet Noise Criterion rating of NC-20 inside the room during full operation.

All acoustic fabric panels were stretched using precision track systems with concealed tensioning splines, guaranteeing razor-sharp seams across fifteen meters of wall.

The Result

The Velvet Cinema stands as a benchmark of residential acoustic engineering and bespoke interior luxury in New Delhi. It proves that technical acoustic perfection does not require compromising on sumptuous material beauty and timeless architectural poise.

It provides the homeowner with a world-class private screening venue that rivals commercial Hollywood mixing stages in performance and surpasses five-star hotels in comfort.

The client frequently hosts exclusive premiere screenings for fellow directors, all of whom marvel at the room's extraordinary acoustic neutrality and sumptuous luxury.
