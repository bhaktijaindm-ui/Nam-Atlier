Master Bedroom and Walk-in Wardrobe

The Dressing Room

Haute Couture Display Architecture in Fluted Glass, Brass and Marble
Location Mumbai, India
Year Completed 2025
Enclosed Area 2,100 SQ FT
Execution Duration 8 Months
Architectural Service Master Bedroom and Walk-in Wardrobe

“Designed as an intimate private gallery for a prominent fashion patron in Mumbai, The Dressing Room transforms daily wardrobe selection into an evocative luxury boutique ritual. Featuring floor-to-ceiling climate-controlled vitrines, a monolithic Calacatta Viola island, and hand-finished bronze garment hardware, the suite blends museum conservation with residential luxury.”

The Brief

The client, an international fashion entrepreneur and collector of vintage couture, required a dedicated 2,100-square-foot dressing pavilion within her sea-facing duplex in Bandra, Mumbai. The space was commissioned to celebrate an extensive collection of rare haute couture garments, bespoke shoes, luxury timepieces, and fine jewelry, treating wardrobe storage as a museum-grade exhibition.

Key requirements included strict humidity and UV climate control to protect delicate silks and leathers against Mumbai's corrosive coastal climate, an integrated beauty vanity station with professional-grade color-rendering illumination, and a lounge area for outfit styling.

The patron also requested private security provisions: integrating biometric access control, hidden floor safes for fine jewelry, and an acoustic sound-lock barrier to separate the dressing pavilion from the master bedroom.

The Challenge

Mumbai's persistent 85% relative humidity and airborne salinity pose severe threats to fine fabrics, natural leathers, and metal fittings. Standard wardrobes often trap moisture, leading to mildew, leather degradation, and tarnished hardware.

Our engineering mandate was to integrate a closed-loop de-humidification and micro-climate filtration system inside each individual wardrobe vitrine without creating visible ducting, noisy compressors, or draft currents that could stir dust onto delicate garments.

Structurally, anchoring heavy four-meter-tall steel and glass wardrobe portals to lightweight internal partitions demanded structural floor-to-ceiling steel unistrut uprights concealed within drywall cavities.

The Concept

We conceptualized the space as 'The Haute Couture Gallery'—a luxurious, private fashion boutique organized symmetrically around an expressive natural stone island. Wardrobes are treated not as opaque storage closets, but as illuminated vitrines framed in ultra-slim walnut profiles and fluted smoked glass.

The spatial layout establishes distinct curation zones: active daily wardrobe along primary circulation corridors, long-hanging couture and evening wear in airtight climate-controlled bays, and a dedicated vanity beauty alcove positioned to capture natural morning daylight.

The atmosphere balances boutique glamour with residential warmth: plush silk-wool carpeting cushions every step, while rich marble veining and warm brass hardware create an air of timeless sophistication.

Space by Space

Access is through double-leaf walnut pivot doors lined with leather. The central gallery is anchored by a four-meter-long bespoke island carved from dramatic Calacatta Viola marble, its rich cabernet veining contrasting with soft ivory carpeting underfoot. The island houses thirty velvet-lined drawers with glass top viewing panels for jewelry and watch winders.

Flanking the room, twelve illuminated wardrobe bays feature integrated garment rods with hidden wiring that powers low-voltage LED strip lights beneath the rails. At the far end, an arched vanity station features a three-panel dressing mirror with concealed touch-dimming halo lighting, paired with a low-slung mohair velvet ottoman.

A discreet shoe pavilion accommodates over one hundred pairs on illuminated angled leather shelves, complete with custom boot shapers and cedarwood lining to ensure perpetual freshness.

Materials and Finishes

The palette balances dramatic natural stone with rich timber and soft textiles. Calacatta Viola marble from Carrara provides rich purple-black veining across a creamy brecciated background. Wardrobe framework is milled from quarter-sawn American walnut treated with non-yellowing natural oils.

Interior drawer linings utilize Italian micro-suede and vegetable-tanned saddle leather hand-stitched at the seams. Metal rails and shelf brackets were custom-extruded in solid brass and hand-finished with an unlacquered satin brush.

Flooring is covered in plush hand-knotted New Zealand wool and mulberry silk carpeting, providing a luxurious, cloud-like underfoot texture that encourages barefoot comfort.

Light and Atmosphere

Illumination was calibrated to high-end fashion runway standards. All wardrobe vitrines incorporate custom 98 CRI (Color Rendering Index) linear LED profiles operating at 3000K, ensuring that fabric tones, gemstone colors, and makeup pigments appear completely true to life without hue distortion.

Subtle motion sensors illuminate individual wardrobe compartments as doors glide open, while ceiling-recessed pin-spot fixtures cast narrow 15-degree pools of light onto the marble island, making jewelry and luxury accessories sparkle like museum artifacts.

At the vanity station, side-mounted vertical diffuse light columns flank the mirror, providing balanced 2700K beauty illumination that eliminates facial shadows for flawless makeup application.

Craft and Execution

Execution required master cabinetmaking tolerances of within one millimeter across all four-meter-tall glass and timber doors. Specialized German pivot hardware with soft-close fluid dampers was concealed inside top and bottom transoms, completely eliminating visible frame hinges.

The climate system utilizes continuous micro-filtration units equipped with HEPA filters and desiccant dehumidifiers concealed in the ceiling void, maintaining an unvarying 50% relative humidity and 22°C temperature inside all vitrines year-round.

All drawers feature concealed Blumotion soft-close runners with synchronized full-extension mechanisms, rated for over 50 kilograms of smooth load-bearing performance.

The Result

The Dressing Room stands as one of Mumbai's most exquisite private wardrobe sanctuaries. It elevates the daily routine of dressing into an artful, inspiring ritual, while offering total conservation-grade protection for rare fashion pieces.

It highlights NAM Atelier’s mastery of bespoke millwork, micro-climate civil integration, and couture interior design.

For the fashion patron, it is an inspiring private haven that balances museum-grade preservation with five-star domestic luxury.
